FAQ
What is ALEK?

ALEK is an AI + Web3 utility project. The current ALK token is deployed on Ethereum Sepolia for testing.

What is ALK?

ALK is the ERC-20 token used by the ALEK project. Current supply is fixed at 1,000,000 ALK with 18 decimals.

Is this Mainnet?

No. The current contract is on Sepolia testnet. Mainnet deployment is planned only after testing and security work.

Can I connect from iPhone?

Yes. The site supports mobile wallet flows and OKX Connect. App-to-browser return behavior can depend on the wallet and iOS; the site also restores sessions where supported.

Can I send ALK?

Yes, on Sepolia. The dashboard includes a Send ALK flow that asks your wallet to approve the ERC-20 transfer.

Will ALEK ask for my seed phrase?

Never. A legitimate DApp should not ask for your seed phrase or private key. Only approve transactions you understand inside your wallet.

How will ALEK make money?

The intended model is real product revenue from AI/Web3 services and premium features, with ALK used for utility where appropriate.

Where can I inspect the contract?

Use the Verified Contract section or the Blockscout link. Always compare the address before interacting.

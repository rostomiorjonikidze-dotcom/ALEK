FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ALEK?

ALEK is an AI + Web3 utility project building practical AI-powered products around the ALK token.

How many ALK tokens are there?

The current Sepolia contract has a fixed total supply of 1,000,000 ALK with 18 decimals.

What network is ALEK on?

The current deployment is on Ethereum Sepolia testnet for development and testing.

How can I connect my wallet?

Use the Connect Wallet button. Desktop wallets can connect through their browser provider; on mobile, use the wallet app's DApp browser for the most reliable connection.

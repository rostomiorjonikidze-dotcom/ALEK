FAQ

What is ALK?

ALK is the utility token of the ALEK project. The current contract is a fixed-supply ERC-20 on Ethereum Sepolia.


Is this mainnet?

No. This website currently points to the Sepolia testnet contract. Mainnet will require a separate deployment.


How do I connect on iPhone?

Use the Connect Wallet button and choose “Open in OKX Wallet”. The OKX app's DApp browser can then connect the wallet to ALEK.
